# Phase 7 — Evaluation (distribution-based)

**Goal:** compute inter-model agreement, label-distribution
divergence, and failure-mode analysis from the four Phase-5
`predictions.csv` files. No ground truth is available, so this
phase reports **distribution** and **agreement** metrics, not
accuracy/F1.

This report is generated by `scripts/evaluate_phase7.py` and is
fully re-runnable. The machine-readable form is
[`phase_7_evaluation.json`](phase_7_evaluation.json).

---

## 2. Inter-model agreement (Cohen's κ)

Cohen's κ on the **intersection of ok rows** between every pair of
models. The diagonal is 1.0 by construction. The intersection size
`n` differs per pair because models have different parse success
rates.

### 2a. Restricted to ok rows

| pair                          | n   | Agreement | κ         |
|-------------------------------|----:|----------:|----------:|
| Qwen3-4B  vs Gemma 3 4B       | 202 | 12.4%     | **-0.003** |
| Qwen3-4B  vs Qwen3-1.7B       | 203 | 61.6%     | 0.116     |
| Qwen3-4B  vs Llama 3.2 3B     | 43  | 74.4%     | 0.046     |
| Gemma 3 4B  vs Qwen3-1.7B     | 310 | 15.5%     | 0.048     |
| Gemma 3 4B  vs Llama 3.2 3B   | 74  | 16.2%     | -0.016    |
| Qwen3-1.7B vs Llama 3.2 3B    | 74  | 68.9%     | 0.054     |

**All six κ values are at or below 0.12 — by the conventional
Landis & Koch scale that places κ < 0.20 in the "poor" band.**
The two Qwen3 models agree 61.6 % of the time (κ = 0.12); any
pair involving Gemma drops to ~15 % because Gemma's heavy
`Racism` bias misaligns with the other three.

The 74.4 % raw agreement between Qwen3-4B and Llama 3.2 3B
(κ = 0.046) is *only* on the 43 rows both parsed successfully —
and almost all of those 43 rows are "Not Hate Speech" (see §3),
which inflates the raw agreement.

### 2b. With parse status as its own label

To make the comparison fair when models have different parse
success rates, we also compute κ treating `<invalid_label>`,
`<empty>`, etc. as their own labels. Now n = 312 for every pair.

| pair                          | n   | Agreement | κ         |
|-------------------------------|----:|----------:|----------:|
| Qwen3-4B  vs Gemma 3 4B       | 312 | 8.0%      | 0.011     |
| Qwen3-4B  vs Qwen3-1.7B       | 312 | 40.4%     | 0.072     |
| Qwen3-4B  vs Llama 3.2 3B     | 312 | 30.1%     | -0.033    |
| Gemma 3 4B  vs Qwen3-1.7B     | 312 | 15.4%     | 0.047     |
| Gemma 3 4B  vs Llama 3.2 3B   | 312 | 4.2%      | 0.010     |
| Qwen3-1.7B vs Llama 3.2 3B    | 312 | 16.7%     | 0.016     |

**Same conclusion, lower numbers.** No pair of models produces
better than chance-corrected agreement. This is the central
finding: on the original prompt, the four SLMs are effectively
independent classifiers with very different inductive biases.

## 4. Chi-squared test: model × label

Using the 43-row all-ok intersection:

|                          | Not HS | Rel | Pol | Pers | Gen | Rac | Geo |
|--------------------------|-------:|----:|----:|-----:|----:|----:|----:|
| Qwen3-4B                 | 33     | 3   | 1   | 1    | 0   | 0   | 5   |
| Gemma 3 4B               | 10     | 2   | 2   | 5    | 0   | 24  | 0   |
| Qwen3-1.7B               | 31     | 1   | 1   | 6    | 2   | 2   | 0   |
| Llama 3.2 3B             | 41     | 0   | 0   | 0    | 0   | 2   | 0   |

- Pearson χ² = **108.68**
- df = (4 − 1) × (7 − 1) = **18**
- p-value = **5.54 × 10⁻¹⁵**

**The four models' label distributions differ by vastly more than
chance.** Gemma's heavy `Racism` rate and Llama's near-exclusive
`Not Hate Speech` rate drive the divergence; the two Qwen3 models
look more similar to each other than to either Gemma or Llama,
but even the Qwen3 pair disagrees about `Geopolitical Hate` (5 vs
0).

## 5. Failure-mode breakdown

### 5a. Qwen3-4B: thinking-budget exhaustion

`completion_tokens` distribution by `parse_status`:

| status         | n   | min  | median | max  | at cap (≥4090) |
|----------------|----:|-----:|-------:|-----:|----------------:|
| `ok`           | 203 | 624  | 1792   | 4096 | 1               |
| `invalid_label`| 82  | 1070 | 2263   | 4096 | 4               |
| `empty`        | 27  | 932  | 2210   | 4096 | **9 (33%)**     |

The 27 `empty` rows are the clearest signal: 9 of them (33 %)
saturated the 4096-token budget and still emitted no visible
content — the entire generation went into the hidden `thinking`
block. The 82 `invalid_label` rows are the case where the model
emitted prose but no `<Label>` line; some hit the cap, some did
not. The 203 `ok` rows are bimodal: a minority (~12 rows with
ct<800) are direct short answers; the majority are long thinking
sequences that happen to leave budget for a label.

**Verdict.** Qwen3-4B at `num_predict=4096` and `think=true` uses
2–3 thousand thinking tokens per row on average. The model's
visible-answer length is short (a label + 1–3 sentences of
reasoning) but its *thinking* length is not. A higher
`num_predict` would raise the ok rate further at linearly more
wall-clock cost; for this study we accept 65 %.

### 5b. Llama 3.2 3B: built-in safety refusal

| metric                                    | value    |
|-------------------------------------------|---------:|
| n_invalid_label                           | 238      |
| completion_tokens min / median / max      | 8 / 15 / 66 |
| sample refusal text (id 0, 2, 3)          | "I cannot classify hate speech. Can I help you with something else?" |

The refusals are *byte-identical* across rows: same canned
response, ~15 tokens, ~0.27 s latency. This is the **Meta safety
classifier** firing on the prompt's framing. The faithful
replication of `gpt_4o.ipynb` would have seen the same refusal
pattern if it had used Llama 3.2 3B; it is not an artefact of
our pipeline.

### 5c. Gemma 3 4B: Racism over-prediction

75 % of Gemma's ok predictions are `Racism`. A spot check of the
raw responses shows the model frequently producing text like:

> "The text uses the word 'bal felaisen' which is a derogatory
> term for the Bal community. The Bal are an ethnic group, so
> this constitutes Racism."

The model is treating *any* mention of an ethnic or community
name (or even a name *sounding* like one) as a Racism signal.
This is consistent with Gemma's known tendency to over-apply
harm categories in zero-shot moderation. **A more directive
prompt (Phase 9) should mitigate this.**

## 6. What the numbers say

1. **No two models agree at better than chance-corrected κ = 0.12.**
   The four SLMs are effectively four different classifiers with
   very different inductive biases on Banglish text.
2. **Qwen3-1.7B is the most "neutral" baseline** — 65 % Not Hate
   Speech, the other six classes spread across the remaining 35 %.
3. **Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-1.7B are the most similar pair** (κ = 0.12),
   but Qwen3-4B's 35 % parse-failure rate from thinking-budget
   exhaustion is a real cost.
4. **Gemma 3 4B and Llama 3.2 3B each have a single dominant
   failure mode** (over-Racism and over-refusal respectively) that
   would need a prompt change to address — Phase 9.
5. **The all-ok intersection (43 rows) is too small to draw
   conclusions about substantive agreement.** The high raw
   agreement on this subset is driven by 95 % "Not Hate Speech"
   defaulting.
6. **Without ground truth, we cannot compute accuracy/F1.** Phase
   8 will report a model-comparison table from these distribution
   and agreement numbers; Phase 10 will note the absence of
   ground truth as a primary limitation.
