# Phase 6 — Validation Report

**Goal:** confirm that the four Phase 5 `predictions.csv` files
match the schema and data-quality contract documented in
[METRICS.md](../METRICS.md) and `src/utils.py:RESULT_CSV_COLUMNS`.
No inference calls, no model changes.

This report is generated by `scripts/validate_phase6.py`, which is
re-runnable. The machine-readable form is
[`phase_6_validation.json`](phase_6_validation.json).

**Overall result:** ✅ **37/37 checks passed across 4 models.** No
data corrections required.

## What's next: Phase 7

Now that the four CSVs are known good, Phase 7 computes
**distribution-based** evaluation metrics:

- Per-model label distribution (counts and fractions of all 7
  labels, including the `invalid_label` and `empty` carve-outs).
- Inter-model agreement on the rows all four models parsed
  successfully (intersection of ok-rows).
- Inter-model disagreement (Cohen's κ between every pair).
- Statistical sanity checks: chi-squared test of independence
  between model and label.
- Failure-mode breakdown for Qwen3-4B and Llama 3.2 3B.

Output goes to `reports/phase_7_evaluation.md`. This is the
primary scientific section of the paper, since we do not have
ground truth.
